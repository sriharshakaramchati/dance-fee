Privacy

This site is used by Soundarya Dance Class to track monthly class fees.

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We use them only to show your own fee status and due date, and to send you fee reminder emails.

Your details are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the class teacher. We do not sell or share them with anyone else. We do not receive your Google password.

To have your details removed, ask the teacher.
